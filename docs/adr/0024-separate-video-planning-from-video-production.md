# Separate video planning from video production

Tuolin adds `$tuolin-video-planner` as an independent user-facing agent for goal discovery, provider-neutral shot planning, confirmed verbatim narration, and automatic SRT delivery, while preserving `$tuolin-video-workflow` for the complete Dreamina generation and assembly workflow. This supersedes the sole-video-entrypoint parts of ADR 0001 and ADR 0003: the two agents share formal knowledge sources but own separate read interfaces, run directories, confirmation flows, state machines, and terminal states so the lightweight planning path never incurs public-trend research or provider execution responsibilities. They may share business-neutral media inspection, authorized asset access, atomic-file, and SRT-validation primitives, but neither workflow imports the other's business stages or appears as a mode flag on the other.
